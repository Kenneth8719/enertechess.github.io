Company Profile

China Enertech Industrial Development Co., Ltd. is a leading international company specializing in the research, development, and manufacturing of high-performance photovoltaic (PV) renewable energy and energy storage systems. Founded in April 2017 and headquartered in Hong Kong, the company has rapidly grown into a key player in the global renewable energy industry through continuous innovation and strategic expansion.

Enertech initially focused on the photovoltaic (PV) sector, establishing a solid technical foundation in materials science and engineering applications. In 2020, the company strategically expanded into the high-growth energy storage systems sector. Leveraging its core technical expertise, it manufactures and sells high-quality PV modules, inverters, and energy storage system products.

These solutions are widely recognized for their exceptional energy conversion efficiency, stable system performance, and long-lasting durability, meeting the stringent requirements of modern clean energy systems.
